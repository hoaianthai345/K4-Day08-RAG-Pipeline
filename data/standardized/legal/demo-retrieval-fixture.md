# E-commerce support guide

## Payment methods

Payment methods include card payment, bank transfer, electronic wallet, and cash on delivery when the order is eligible. Customers should review the available payment methods at checkout before confirming an order.

## Seller listing regulations

Seller listing regulations require accurate product information, lawful products, and truthful images. A seller must not publish prohibited, counterfeit, or misleading listings.

## Order tracking guide

The order tracking guide explains how a buyer can open the order details screen, view the delivery status, and contact support if the tracking information does not update.
